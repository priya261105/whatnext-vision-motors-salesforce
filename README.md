**WhatNext Vision Motors – Salesforce Project**

📌 Project Overview

**WhatNext Vision Motors** is a Salesforce-based CRM solution designed to streamline vehicle sales and customer management operations.

The system helps manage customers, vehicles, vehicle orders, dealers, test drives, and service requests through Salesforce automation and centralized data management.

It helps in:

* Customer Management
* Vehicle & Inventory Management
* Vehicle Order Management
* Dealer Management
* Test Drive Scheduling
* Vehicle Service Request Management
